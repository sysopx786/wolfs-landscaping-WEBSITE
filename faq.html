Frequently asked questions

Getting started

How do I get an estimate?

Fill out the form on our home page or call 610-357-1098. We're notified of every request.

How fast will you get back to me?

We reply to estimate requests within 1–2 days by text or email. If you call and we miss you, leave a voicemail and we'll call back.

Is the estimate free?

Yes. Requesting an estimate costs nothing.

Our company

Where do you work?

Phoenixville and the surrounding Chester County area. Not sure if we cover your address? Send a request and we'll tell you.

Who will be doing the work?

We're owner-operated, so you deal directly with the people on your property.

What services do you offer?

Lawn care, landscape design and installation, hardscaping, retaining walls, drainage and grading, sod and seeding, seasonal cleanup, tree work and snow removal. See the Services menu for details.

Scheduling and weather

What happens if weather changes my appointment?

We'll let you know and reschedule so you aren't left guessing.

Can I book a one-time job?

Yes. Many jobs are one-time projects such as a cleanup, a wall or a regrade.

Still have a question? Send us a request or call 610-357-1098.
