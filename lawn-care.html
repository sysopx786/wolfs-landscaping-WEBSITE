Lawn Care & Maintenance

Mowing and upkeep, handled on schedule.

Request a Free Estimate Call 610-357-1098

A good lawn doesn't take magic. It takes someone who shows up when they say they will and does the job right every time. That's what we aim for on every yard we take care of.

We handle regular mowing and trimming, ongoing groundskeeping, and the small stuff that keeps a property looking cared for. If the weather pushes your day, we text you and reschedule. You shouldn't have to chase us.

What's included

  • Lawn mowing and edging
  • String trimming around beds, fences and trees
  • Blowing off driveways, walks and patios
  • Groundskeeping and general yard work
  • Ongoing landscape management for your property

How we work

Tricky yard? No problem.

Slopes, tight gates and odd corners are part of the job. We'll look at your lawn first and tell you what to expect.

We keep you in the loop.

Weather delay or schedule change? You'll hear from us before we're late, not after.

Simple, fair pricing.

You get a clear price up front, based on your actual property.

Common questions

How often will you mow?

Most customers choose weekly or every other week during the growing season. We'll suggest what fits your lawn and budget.

Do I have to be home?

No. As long as we can get to the lawn, we'll do the work and let you know when we're finished.

Can you take care of leaves in the fall?

Yes. See our seasonal cleanup service.
