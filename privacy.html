Privacy Policy

Draft for owner review. This describes how the site currently behaves. Have it reviewed before relying on it.

What we collect

If you send an estimate request, we receive what you put in the email: name, phone, email (optional), address or town, the service you want and your message. We use it only to reply to your request and to do the work you ask about.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not set its own cookies and does not run analytics or advertising trackers.

Third parties

Fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads. The estimate form does not send data to a server. It opens your own email app with your request written out, and nothing is sent until you press Send in your email app. Links to Google reviews take you to Google, which has its own policy.

Sharing

We don't sell your information.

Contact

Questions or requests to delete your information: call 610-357-1098.
