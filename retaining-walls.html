Retaining Walls

Solve the slope. Add the structure.

Request a Free Estimate Call 610-357-1098

A slope can cause real headaches: washed-out soil, a yard you can't use, or a bed that won't hold together. A well-built retaining wall fixes the problem and can make the whole yard look better.

We build walls that do their job first. Then we make sure they look good doing it.

What's included

  • Retaining wall construction
  • Stone and block walls
  • Walls combined with steps
  • Leveling and shaping slopes for usable space
  • Drainage planning so the wall holds up

How we work

We look at the whole slope.

Water, soil and weight all matter. We plan for them before we build.

Drainage comes first.

A wall that can't drain won't last. We build with water in mind.

Straight talk on cost.

We'll explain what drives the price and what your options are.

Common questions

Do I need a wall or just grading?

Sometimes regrading is enough. We'll tell you honestly which one fits.

Can a wall make my yard more usable?

Yes. Walls can turn a steep slope into a flat area you can use.

Can steps be built into the wall?

Yes, and it's a common way to connect upper and lower areas.
