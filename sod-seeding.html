Sod & Seeding

A new lawn, started the right way.

Starting a lawn from scratch or repairing a thin, patchy one comes down to good prep. Get the ground ready and the right grass in, and the rest gets easier.

Seed costs less and takes patience. Sod gives you a green lawn right away. We'll help you choose, then do the work.

Common questions

How soon can I use my new lawn?

It depends on seed or sod and the weather. We'll go over it before we start.

When should I seed?

Early fall is often a good window. Spring can work too. We'll recommend timing for your yard.

Can you fix just part of my lawn?

Yes. Patching bare areas is a common job.
